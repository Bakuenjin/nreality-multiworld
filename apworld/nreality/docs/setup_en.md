# NReality Multiworld Setup Guide

## Requirements

- MultiworldGG
- `nreality.apworld`
- Your own copy of NReality 6g2: `NReality6g2.zip` from [n.infunity.com](https://n.infunity.com/). Game files are not distributed.

## Installation

1. Double-click `nreality.apworld` (or use "Install APWorld" in the MultiworldGG launcher).
2. Start **NReality Client** from the launcher. Your browser opens the game page.
3. First start only: choose `NReality6g2.zip` on the page. The client builds the game from it and downloads the Ruffle
   Flash emulator once (internet required). Nothing else needs to be installed.

## Playing

Connect in the console on the right with `/connect <address>` and enter your slot name, or click your slot on the room
page. Click the game, choose "play game" and an open episode. Greyed-out numbers are episodes of your seed that are
still locked. Chat, `!hint` and the other commands work in the same console.

Level completions are stored in the browser until the server confirms them, so they survive connection losses.

## Generating

Whoever generates the multiworld needs only the `.apworld`, not the game. Upload the generated archive to multiworld.gg
to host it.
